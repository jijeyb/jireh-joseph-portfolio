    About me

    Holding a degree in Electronics Engineering, I specialize in Graphic Design, Automation, and Artificial Intelligence to build forward-looking technical solutions.

    Rather than treating engineering, UX, and graphic design as separate silos, I approach every project as a single, unified system where all parts reinforce each other.

    My work sits at the intersection of intelligent backend algorithms, clean GUI code, and intentional visual design, connecting complex logic with user-facing interfaces.

    Driven by technical ambition and aesthetic care, I focus on delivering complex backend architecture wrapped in clean, functional, and striking visual design.

    I bridge the space between optimized machine learning pipelines and intuitive design, proving that clean code and careful visuals are most powerful when built together.

    How we work

    Four steps from first message to something running. No mystery in between.

    1. 01

      Brief

      You tell me the goal, the audience, and what done looks like. I ask the questions that save time later.

    2. 02

      Plan

      I map the visuals and the workflow before building anything, so scope and timing are clear up front.

    3. 03

      Build

      Design and automation come together. You see real progress early and steer it while it is still easy to change.

    4. 04

      Launch

      We ship it, check it in real use, and I hand over what you need to run it with confidence.
